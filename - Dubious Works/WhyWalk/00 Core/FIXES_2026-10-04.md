# WhyWalk — 2026-10-04 bug pass

Three symptoms from the riding-session log. Two were single defects with a
clear cause; the third now reports itself instead of being guessed at.

The log also settled two background questions: Devilish Guar Riding is fully
gone from the load order (plugin and scripts), which is why positioning and
facing came right, and the guar being ridden is therefore a **vanilla** guar
matched by the `"guar"` pattern — a *steered* mount, not a free ride. That
distinction turns out to be the whole of symptom 1.

---

## Symptom 1 — guar T-posed while sliding and rotating

**WhyWalk has never animated the mount.** The manifest said so plainly:
`# No mount-side script.`

`whywalk_global.lua`'s header argued one was unnecessary, and named the single
thing that would justify adding it back:

> visible gait animation fighting (the creature playing a walk cycle in a
> direction it is not moving)

What happened is worse than that, and in the opposite direction. The creature
does not play the *wrong* animation — it plays **none**, and renders at bind
pose. The assumption was that the AI would still drive the gait and we would be
overriding the result. It does not: OpenMW's character controller picks a
creature's locomotion group from the movement it is asked to perform, and **a
teleport is not movement**. The controller is never asked for anything, never
selects a group, and an actor with no active group is a T-pose.

This is RESEARCH §1.10's p37z note arriving the expensive way — teleporting a
mount every frame reimplements gait, root motion, collision, gravity and
pathing, badly. p37z's `controls.movement` approach remains the better
architecture and is the eventual answer. This fix is the smaller one that
matches Devilish Guar Riding, which is the behaviour the mod is tuned against.

**New: `scripts/WhyWalk/whywalk_mount.lua`**, registered `CUSTOM`, attached with
`addScript` on mount and removed on dismount, so an unridden creature carries no
code — the condition the header set. It plays one looping locomotion group
chosen by the gait the global script already computes, at `PRIORITY.Default`
with no per-frame handler.

Three details worth knowing:

- **Default priority, deliberately.** This is the creature's own locomotion, not
  an override of it. Anything higher wins fights it should lose — a ridden
  creature that is hit should still play its hit reaction, and `Scripted` would
  stop its death animation running at all (RESEARCH §2.2).
- **Free ride is excluded.** There the creature keeps its AI and the engine
  drives it, so it already animates; adding a second source of groups would be
  the gait fighting the header warned about. Only steered mounts get the script.
- **The first gait is not sent on attach.** `addScript` is deferred to the next
  frame, so an immediate `sendEvent` would land before the handler exists and be
  dropped. The cached `lastRiderState` is cleared instead, which makes the next
  `onUpdate` treat the current gait as a change and broadcast it once the script
  is live.

## Symptom 2 — camera pulled back to facing

**Mine, from last pass.** I removed the `not firstPerson` gate on the rider's
yaw and wrote in the comment that "teleport's rotation is the BODY's, not the
camera's". That is wrong. In OpenMW the player's body yaw and the third-person
camera yaw are the same number — mouse-look turns the actor. Writing an absolute
yaw once per frame overwrites every mouse movement before it can be seen, and
the view snaps back to the mount's heading. The old gate existed for exactly
this reason.

Gating it again would only move the problem, because the MWScript pin does the
same thing in third person via `player->SetAngle Z`, and third person is the
view this mod exists to support.

The rider still has to turn *with* the mount or they sit sideways through every
corner. Both requirements are met by applying the **change** in heading rather
than its value:

```
playerYaw = playerYaw + (mountYaw - mountYawLastFrame)
```

Mount turns 10° right, rider turns 10° right; whatever the player added with the
mouse is already in `playerYaw` when the delta lands on top of it, so it
survives. Verified at 8 checks including north-crossing wraparound and a 7-turn
sequence — the mouse offset is preserved to 1e-9, where the absolute model
zeroes it.

Zero is a safe "do nothing" sentinel here in a way it is **not** for an absolute
angle: a delta of zero means the heading did not change, so skipping the write
is correct. (An absolute version cannot tell "facing north" from "no request" —
that is the real bug in p37z's pin, RESEARCH §1.13.)

Applies to both pins. The MWScript now reads
`player->GetAngle Z + whywalk_yawdelta` and zeroes the global after use
(consume-on-use), and the `PCGet3rdPerson` gate is gone — a delta is correct in
first person too, and contributes nothing when the mount goes straight.

### This needs a plugin change

The global is **renamed**: `whywalk_angle` → `whywalk_yawdelta`. Same name with
new meaning would have been one less step in the CS, but an un-recompiled plugin
would then apply deltas as absolute headings and snap the rider near-north every
frame. The rename makes a stale plugin fail safe instead.

`bridgeReady()` now probes `whywalk_yawdelta` specifically, so an old plugin is
rejected and the Lua teleport pin takes over, with a message saying what to add.

`WhyWalk_RiderPin_mwscript.txt` is updated. Add the `whywalk_yawdelta` float,
paste the script, **save it so it compiles**, and confirm SCDT is non-empty.

> The copy of `WhyWalk.omwaddon` in the zip you sent is **still uncompiled**
> (`scriptDataSize = 0`, `numFloats = 0`). Since positioning and facing work in
> game, the plugin you are actually running must be a recompiled one that did
> not make it back into the zip. Worth reconciling so the shipped artifact is
> the working one.

## Symptom 3 — player animation flickering when running and jumping

Not fixed; **made measurable**, with the leading cause named.

`ridingAnim.lua` recovers from the engine interrupting the rider pose by
re-issuing it from an `addAnimationEndedHandler`. That recovery *is* a visible
one-frame flicker. The burst guard only speaks up above 5 per second, so a
steady 2–4 per second is currently silent — and that is more than enough to look
like flickering.

"Running and jumping especially" fits: those are the gallop and jump states,
where the per-frame position deltas are largest and the engine is most likely to
decide the pinned, levitating rider is falling and play its own animation over
the pose.

The handler now counts re-issues per ride and prints the rate on dismount when
it exceeds 1/s:

```
[WhyWalk] rider pose was re-issued 47 times in 18.3s (2.6/s). Each one is a
visible flicker: the engine is interrupting the pose, most likely the
fall/levitate animation on a pinned rider. Try turning useLevitation off in
whywalk_shared.lua to confirm.
```

That turns the next log into evidence. **Please ride for a minute, dismount, and
send the line** — the rate distinguishes "occasional recovery, working as
intended" from "the pose is being fought every few frames".

Levitation is the first suspect because WhyWalk applies it itself
(`useLevitation = true`) to stop gravity fighting the pin, and a levitating
actor has its own flying animation that competes with the seated pose. It is a
one-line A/B in `whywalk_shared.lua`.

Also in the frame, from your load order: **FLOW** loads at `#749`, immediately
after `ridingAnim` at `#746`, and both drive player animation; **ReAnimation v3**
ships `animationoverrides.lua` on PLAYER. Neither is implicated by anything in
the log yet, but if the rate comes back low while the flicker stays, they are
where to look next.

## Also fixed: AnimRefresh v4 — affects the whole suite

The log carries this, once per game:

```
[11:04:18.374 E] Not supported handler 'UiModeChanged'
                 in L@0x1[scripts/animrefresh/animrefresh_v4.lua]
```

`UiModeChanged` is an **event**, not an engine handler, and v4 registers it under
`engineHandlers`. OpenMW rejects it outright. The consequence is that v4's
Rest/Travel/Training/Jail refresh — one of the four things the version was
written to add — **has never run**, in any mod shipping it.

Moved to `eventHandlers` here. **This needs porting to CAKE, IED, Take a Seat
and Bardcraft**, which ship the same file at the same shared path; the fix is
correct in whichever copy wins the version guard, but only if they all carry it.

The stale `AnimRefresh_v3.lua` was also still sitting in the folder unreferenced;
removed.
