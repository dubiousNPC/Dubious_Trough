## The clockwise lean: it is not in the maths

I tested for handedness rather than guessing, because "usually too far
clockwise" is a **different complaint from lag** and the two are impossible to
tell apart from inside the game. Lag leans whichever way the path is turning;
a bias leans the same way regardless. If the easing had a handedness bug,
fixing lag would never fix the lean.

Two mirror-image spiral paths through the real code:

```
turning clockwise     : mean signed error  +1.333 deg
turning anticlockwise : mean signed error  -1.333 deg
```

Equal and opposite to three decimals, and `easeHeading` turns the same amount
in both directions to 1e-9. **There is no handedness in the heading code**, so
the clockwise lean cannot come from the easing or the lookahead.

Which leaves the creature as the most likely source — an actor whose facing
the engine also manages, being handed a rotation every frame. That is gone
now. Both symmetry checks are in the suite so this stays settled.

**If it is still leaning clockwise on the activator**, the question that
separates the remaining causes is: *does it lean on the straights too, or only
in bends?* Straights too means a constant offset, which is the mesh's own
forward axis and a one-line fix. Only in bends is residual lag, and the
constants can go further.

## The lookahead stays

It is independent of the creature change, it is measurably better, and the
symmetry tests show it is not what caused the lean:

| turnRate | lookahead | mean | worst | >10° |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.9 | 0 | 5.2 | 74.7 | 18.7% | ← before |
| 1.4 | 350 | 2.3 | 51.7 | 9.9% | ← now |

If you want it out while judging the lean, `lookahead = 0` and
`turnRate = 0.9` in `caravans_db.lua` restores the old behaviour exactly.
