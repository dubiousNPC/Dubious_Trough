# Caravans: heading, root motion, rider pose, turning (2026-11-03c)

All four reported faults were measured in OpenMW 0.52 RC1 with Immersive
Travel's own strider meshes, and each is fixed and re-measured in the engine.

## 1. Facing the wrong way: `math.atan` under LuaJIT (the root cause)

OpenMW runs **LuaJIT (Lua 5.1)**. There `math.atan` takes one argument and
silently ignores a second. The tools here run on Lua 5.4, where
`math.atan(y, x)` is the two-argument form. In OpenMW 0.52,
`math.atan(1, -1) = 0.7854` where 2.3562 was meant.

`caravans_path.headingOf` was `atan(dx, dy)` from the first build, so in the
game every heading was folded into ±90°, pointing the wrong way whenever the
route ran south. Every facing model since (lag filter, lookahead, unwrap,
steering, carrot, mesh offset) was fed that, and every test passed, because
the tests ran on 5.4.

Engine measurement of a real Balmora → Ald-ruhn journey, with the bug:
turn rate up to **8421 °/s**, sideways up to **180°**.

**Fix:** `local atan2 = math.atan2 or math.atan`.

**Guards:**
- Both test files now run with a LuaJIT-faithful one-argument `math.atan`.
  With the bug reintroduced, eight tests fail with the engine's own numbers
  (8100 °/s, 179.9°).
- `tools/check_luajit.py` is in `check_all.sh`. It rejects two-argument atan,
  `//`, bitwise operators and 5.3-only library calls. The rest of WhyWalk
  (Core, Boats) is clean.

## 2. `yawOffset = PI` was wrong: the mesh faces +Y

Three independent measurements of IT's `r/siltstrider.nif`:
- the Head bone is at y = +255 and the mandibles reach y = +695;
- RunForward's root motion runs along +Y;
- filmed from above in the engine, `rotateZ(π/2)` puts the head at +X.

The object yaw is therefore the travel heading, with no offset. That is also
what Immersive Travel does (`mount.facing = atan2(d.x, d.y)`).

## 3. "Jumps forward and back": root motion an activator keeps

IT's `xsiltstrider.kf` moves "Root Bone" +Y by 800 units per loop
(RunForward 300 → 1100 in 1.8 s). OpenMW turns that into movement for actors
only. On an activator the body runs ahead of its origin and snaps back.

Filmed with the object held still, the body's centre swept **125 px** in a
sawtooth. With the in-place copy (root X/Y zeroed, nothing else changed) it
swept **2 px**. This can't be fixed from Lua: `openmw.animation` is local-only
and has no root-motion switch. `tools/build_inplace_strider.py` makes the copy
under `meshes/whywalk/caravans/`. Without it, the strider idles and the log
names the tool.

The gait is now also played at travel speed ÷ natural speed (RunForward
444 u/s, WalkForward 150 u/s, measured from the `.kf`), so the feet keep pace.
That is 3.15× at the 1400 u/s default, re-timed through corners.

## 4. Floating rider, no idle pose: two causes

**The slot was below the floor.** The spawned strider's collision comes from
its *render* mesh: IT's RootCollisionNode sits under "Root Bone", not under
the root, where the engine looks for it. A raycast puts the cabin floor at
**1223.1**. The slot was 1223.0, so every frame the rider started 0.1 below
the floor, fell through it, and came to rest on the body at 991.8. The slots
are now at 1226; placed once, the rider rests on the floor at 1224.1.

**A teleported actor is never grounded.** `mwphysics/actor.cpp`
`updatePosition` sets `mSkipSimulation = true` and `mStandingOnPtr = nullptr`.
With a real floor under the slot, the rider was on the ground in **2%** of
frames while pinned every frame, and **100%** when placed once. So the engine
played the falling animation for the whole ride. With the seated pose off, the
player script sent no pose at all. It now always sends one: `passenger` when
seated, otherwise Core's `stand` (vanilla idle on the vessel layer, above the
fall).

## 5. Nauseating turns: round the corners, slow for the tight ones

The polyline turns 22° per node at the median, about once a second. Each
corner is replaced by a circular arc (radius 1000, never more than 120 units
inside the corner), and the body faces the path's exact tangent. Arcs tighter
than 90 °/s at cruise get a speed profile: brake at 700 u/s², hold on the arc,
recover. The profile is a pure function of distance, as the facing is. The
Turn smoothness setting is removed, because there is no longer a trade-off to
choose.

| over all 47 routes, 60 fps | turn max | sideways mean / max | off the line | time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| carrot 350 (previous, LuaJIT-correct) | 9123 °/s | 2.05° / 178° | 0 | – |
| arcs + speed profile | **90 °/s** | **0.13° / 0.75°** | ≤ 120 | +1.2% |

**In the engine**, on a full Balmora → Ald-ruhn journey (6377 frames):
- turn max **89.5 °/s**;
- sideways **0.11° mean, 1.64° max**;
- rider exactly **1226** above the vehicle origin every frame;
- gait `runforward` at **2.82–3.15×**.

## Verification

`test_caravans` **131** and `test_intercept` **105**, both under LuaJIT maths.
All checkers are clean, including the new `check_luajit`.

## For you to decide

- **Speed.** At the 1400 u/s default the gait plays at 3.15×. Immersive
  Travel's own pace is about 180 u/s, and RunForward's natural pace is 444. A
  default nearer 450 would make the legs look natural, at the cost of journeys
  about 3× as long.
- **Shipping the in-place copy.** It is 2 of IT's files copied verbatim plus
  one patched `.kf`. Check IT's permissions before distributing it. Otherwise
  ship only the tool and have users build the copy.
- **Not exercised here:** Core's pose layer actually playing `stand` (the test
  skeleton has no animations) and the MWScript pin. Those need a real install.
